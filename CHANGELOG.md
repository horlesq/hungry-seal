# Changelog

What changed for players in each release. Newest first.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

The first full release.

**The game**
- Swim, leap and eat your way through three big maps: Seal Bay, the Arctic and the Tropical
  Lagoon. Each has its own regions, from calm beaches to shark-filled open water, caves,
  wrecks, minefields and a deep lair.
- Grow through 8 sizes, from a pup that fits through tiny grottos to a seal that eats sharks.
  Currents block the deep until you're big enough.
- Beat the squid bosses (the Kraken, the Colossal Squid, the Abyssal Squid) by biting them
  after they attack.
- Collect 6 pearls on each map; 3 open the next map.
- Fill the Frenzy meter to eat anything for a few seconds.
- Upgrades, 9 seal skins, missions, achievements, gems and a local top-5.

**A real challenge**
- Hunger builds up over a run: the start is forgiving, but after a few minutes your bar empties
  fast and you have to keep hunting.
- Bigger seals get hungrier faster, and tiny fish fill a big seal less: once you're grown, hunt
  big prey and the predators you've outgrown.
- Sharks, hammerheads and orcas now swim a little faster than you and stalk you when they sense
  you. Boost when you see the "!", or leap out of the water. Sharks also hunt up into the
  lower shallows; only the water near the surface is safe. Even a full-grown seal has to run
  from an orca.
- More jellyfish and mines around a big seal. A big seal shrugs off stings better, though:
  the late game is about hunger and the big hunters.
- Frenzy is a treat again: it charges by how much a meal is worth, and rests for a while after
  each frenzy (the flame dims).
- The Arctic and the Tropical Lagoon are harder than Seal Bay: hungrier water and more hunters.
- Growing to the biggest sizes takes longer (reaching the boss is a real achievement).
- The Arctic's Floe Fields are a calm place to grow before Iceberg Alley, and there are fewer
  penguins (too big for a pup) on the West Shore.
- Pufferfish only puff when you come right up to them, not when you hunt other fish nearby.
- The results screen gives a tip for whatever got you.

**Runs smoothly everywhere**
- Much faster on phones (the interface drew far more than it needed to).
- Loads quicker: smaller art, and skins and bosses load only when needed.
- If a device can't keep up, the game renders a little softer instead of stuttering.
- Install it to your home screen and play offline after the first visit.
- Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop and mobile, with mouse, keyboard or
  touch.
