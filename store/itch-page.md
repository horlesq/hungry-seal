## Title
Hungry Seal

## Short description (tagline)
Eat. Grow. Don't get eaten. A chunky arcade swim-and-eat game starring a very hungry seal.

## Description

You're a seal, and you're always hungry. Your hunger bar never stops draining, so keep eating!
Gobble fish smaller than you, grow bigger, and soon the sharks that chased you are on the menu.

**Features**
- Three big maps to explore: **Seal Bay**, the **Arctic** and the **Tropical Lagoon**, each with
  beaches, reefs, caves, shipwrecks, minefields and a deep, dark lair.
- **Grow through 8 sizes**: squeeze through tiny grottos as a pup, push through strong currents
  once you're big, and eat what used to hunt you.
- **Boss fights** with three giant squids. Dodge the lunge, then bite back!
- **Frenzy mode**: fill the meter and eat *anything* for a few seconds.
- Leap out of the water to catch seabirds.
- Collect **pearls** to open new maps, earn coins for **upgrades**, and dress up your seal with
  **9 skins**: harbor seal, walrus, elephant seal, a pirate and more.
- Missions, achievements and your own top-5 runs.
- Plays on desktop and on phones. Install it to your home screen and it works offline too.

**Controls**
- **Mouse:** the seal swims toward the cursor; hold the button to boost.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to swim, Space or Shift to boost.
- **Touch:** touch anywhere and drag to swim (a joystick appears under your finger); tap Boost
  to dash.
- **Pause:** Esc, P or the pause button.

Made with Phaser, TypeScript and Blender. All sounds are made in code.
